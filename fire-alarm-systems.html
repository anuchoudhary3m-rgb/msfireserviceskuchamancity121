Detailed Overview & Specifications

An early fire alarm warning system is the single most vital component for safeguarding human lives and securing Rajasthan Fire NOC clearance. MS FIRE SERVICE INDUSTRIES designs and installs turn-key conventional and addressable fire alarm systems across Kuchaman City and Didwana. We supply photoelectric smoke detectors, manual call points (break-glass units), multi-zone annunciator control panels, and high-decibel electronic hooters with dual battery backup.
